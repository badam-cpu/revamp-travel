A great Revamp listing does three things: it looks trustworthy at a glance, it sets accurate expectations, and it answers the questions a guest has before they ask. Here's how to get there.

## Lead with strong photos
Your **cover photo** is the single biggest factor in whether someone clicks. Revamp stores a cover image plus a gallery and serves them responsively (sharp on every screen), so quality matters.

- Use bright, real photos of the actual space — no stock imagery, no watermarks.
- Shoot in daylight; show the whole room, then the details.
- Order them like a walkthrough: exterior/hero → main room → bedrooms → bathroom → views/extras.
- Upload several — the more complete the gallery, the fewer doubts a guest has.

## Write an honest, specific title and description
- Say what's genuinely special ("5-minute walk to Republic Square," "wood-burning stove," "quiet courtyard"). Specific beats generic.
- Never inherit or invent star ratings or reviews — Revamp strips imported ratings and never publishes fabricated social proof. Your real reviews build over time (see the Reviews article).
- Keep it scannable: short paragraphs, concrete facts.

## Fill in the details guests filter and worry about
These fields do real work in search and on the listing page:

- **Amenities** and **house rules** (e.g. self check-in, no smoking) — pick from the curated lists so they're searchable.
- **Check-in / checkout times** and **minimum stay** for stays.
- **Property type** (apartment, guesthouse, hotel…), **cleaning fee**, and your **cancellation policy**.
- For tours/experiences: duration, meeting point, languages, what's included/not included.

## Price clearly, in dram
Prices are set and charged in Armenian dram (AMD). Use the per-listing calendar to set seasonal rates and block dates. A guest sees the full breakdown at checkout — accommodation, cleaning fee, and the 10% tax added on top — so there are no surprises.

## Why this matters beyond your page
Revamp publishes rich, accurate listing data to Google and AI trip planners. Complete, honest listings are the ones that get surfaced and cited — so filling everything in is also how new travelers find you.
